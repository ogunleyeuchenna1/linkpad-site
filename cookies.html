Browser cookies and local storage

LinkPad Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This page explains how cookies and similar browser storage relate to the LinkPad website, app, and download links.

Cookies on the LinkPad information site

The static LinkPad product website does not currently set LinkPad login cookies, advertising cookies, or analytics cookies. It is an informational site with links to product downloads and does not require an account to read its pages.

Cookies used by app features

The LinkPad application uses browser storage in its local app interface to remember limited settings such as the paired client identifier and selected keyboard modifier mode. This storage supports the app connection and interface; it is not used by the public marketing pages for cross-site advertising. You can clear site data in your browser or app webview settings, though clearing app storage may require pairing again.

Third-party services

The website is published using GitHub Pages. Download buttons open files stored in Google Drive. Those services may set or read their own cookies and collect technical or account information under their own privacy and cookie policies. LinkPad does not control third-party cookies. Review the provider policies and your browser's privacy controls when using those services.

Managing cookies and storage

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies and clear site data in their privacy settings. Blocking all cookies or storage may affect features operated by GitHub or Google, and clearing LinkPad app storage can remove the saved pairing identifier. Browser controls vary by device and browser version; consult the browser's help documentation for the exact steps.

Policy updates and contact

If LinkPad later adds analytics, advertising, or other non-essential cookies, this policy should be updated before those technologies are introduced. Questions about this policy can be sent to ogunleyeuchenna@gmail.com. Also see the Privacy Policy.
