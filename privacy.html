LinkPad Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy describes the information handled by the LinkPad website and the LinkPad Windows and Android applications. LinkPad is designed for direct transfers on a local network and does not operate a LinkPad cloud account or cloud storage service.

Information handled by the app

When you pair devices, the app uses the pairing code and device names to establish and display a connection. Text and files you choose to send are transferred to the paired device over the local Wi-Fi network. The PC runs the LinkPad server for that session; shared files are stored temporarily on that PC and are removed after their 30-minute availability period or when the server shuts down. File names, file sizes, connection status, and transfer events may be held in memory while the app is running so the interface can work.

LinkPad's current local connection uses HTTP and does not provide end-to-end encryption. Anyone with access to an untrusted network may be able to observe or interfere with local traffic. Use LinkPad only on a trusted private network, and do not send information that requires stronger confidentiality.

Website and download providers

The public site is hosted by GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub may process technical information such as request details, IP address, browser, and security logs under its own policies. Download links currently open Google Drive files; Google may process download, device, and account information or use cookies according to Google's own privacy terms. LinkPad does not control those providers' systems or retention practices.

The LinkPad site itself does not currently use advertising pixels, analytics scripts, or a LinkPad login cookie. See the separate Cookie Policy for more detail about browser storage and third-party services.

Purpose, sharing, and retention

Data sent through the app is used to deliver the file, note, or keyboard action you requested. LinkPad does not intentionally upload that content to a LinkPad-operated cloud service or sell personal information. Files shared through the PC server are temporary and expire after 30 minutes. We do not claim control over copies that a recipient chooses to save, screenshots, operating-system download records, or data handled by GitHub and Google.

Your choices and security

  • Only pair devices you recognize, and keep the PC and phone on a trusted network.
  • Share only the files and text needed for the task; avoid credentials and highly sensitive data.
  • Close the PC app when you have finished sharing.
  • Use browser and device settings to manage provider cookies, download history, and saved files.

No method of software transmission or storage is perfectly secure. The app is provided without a cloud backup; keep independent copies of important data.

Children and policy changes

LinkPad is a general-purpose utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children. This policy may be updated when the website or app changes. The date at the top indicates the latest editorial update. Continued use after an update means you have had an opportunity to review the current policy.
