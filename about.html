The people and purpose behind LinkPad

About LinkPad

LinkPad is a practical bridge between a Windows PC and the phone already in your hand. It brings nearby file sharing, text notes, and phone-keyboard input together in one local Wi-Fi app.

Why LinkPad exists

Moving a picture, document, or short piece of text between a phone and a computer should not require emailing it to yourself, searching for a cable, signing up for a cloud account, or typing the same information twice. LinkPad was created to make those small handoffs more direct.

Open LinkPad on a Windows PC and pair a phone by scanning the QR code. Android users can use the LinkPad app; iPhone users can scan with the Camera app and continue in the browser. Once connected on the same Wi-Fi network, devices can send files and notes in either direction. A phone can also act as a convenient keyboard for the focused Windows application.

The product is designed around local connections rather than a LinkPad cloud account. That keeps the workflow simple, while also making it important to use the app only on a trusted network: LinkPad's current local transfer connection uses HTTP and is not end-to-end encrypted.

What LinkPad can do

  • Share files up to 2 GB between a Windows PC and a paired phone over local Wi-Fi.
  • Show upload progress and allow shared files to be downloaded repeatedly until they expire.
  • Send text notes, copy received notes on Android, and type on the PC using the phone's keyboard.
  • Pair Android with a QR scan, or connect an iPhone through its browser.
